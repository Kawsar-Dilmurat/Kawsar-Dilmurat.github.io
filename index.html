About

About Me

I'm a Data Analyst with an MPS in Informatics from Northeastern University (GPA 3.87), completed December 2025, on top of a Bachelor's in International Economics and Trade.


Currently a Data Analyst at ZJW Inc. (on STEM OPT), where I analyze e-commerce sales, pricing, and inventory data — tracking category-level performance, cleaning and standardizing product catalog data, and building weekly reports that inform pricing and purchasing decisions.


Outside of day-to-day work, I apply the same rigor to independent projects — see the case studies below. Before data analytics, I spent 3 years in financial operations — reconciliation, reporting, process — and that habit of tracing every number back to its source is what still shapes how I approach any dataset I pick up.


My working method is simple: define the business question before touching a dataset, back every number with a chart or table, and report exceptions honestly instead of smoothing them into a cleaner story. I'm trilingual — Mandarin, English, and Uyghur, and actively looking for Data Analyst / Business Analyst roles.
